The World at Large — Faerûn in 2511 DR

The world as it stands a thousand years after the end: three cities holding on the Sword Coast and the Silver Marches, and everywhere the cities are not, the Badlands — blighted ground, hatestorm country, demon enclaves, the claimed lands of the Kingdom of the Undead, and wilds no one claims at all. One grey sky lies over all of it. The Fog of Life covers the whole surface of the world; it does not cross the mythals, it does not go underground, and no sun and no stars have been seen through it since the day it came.

The ground itself is traced from the old survey of Faerûn — every creek of the coast, the Sea of Fallen Stars in its true shape, Anauroch at its real extent, every range and river in its true course — and drawn over with the thousand years: blight scars and dead ground in the Badlands, twisted growth in the wilds, ember cracks in the demon enclaves, all of it under the Fog’s grey. Zoom in and the survey deepens in stages: countries and great waters first, then towns, forests, ranges, and rivers, then the scars themselves and the held cities’ own works — the Bastion’s walls, the Cut, and the Heart on the water; Silverymoon’s armored works and the Moonbridge; Sundabar’s walls and mines.

The held cities stand inside their mythals, where the air is their own. Everywhere else the Fog lies grey and untouchable over the land, and the land itself is the Badlands: everywhere the city is not. Forests, plains, mountains, and dead kingdoms keep their own names inside it. Beyond the walls the war never ended — Life, in all its kinds, against the demons and against the Kingdom of the Undead, and the Warforged fighting as a side of their own.

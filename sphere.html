Since the APOC — the Closing, called the Cut

A thousand years after the apocalypse, the placements above stand exactly as the record sets them. What changed is not where things are — it is what can reach them.

Scarred shut

The sphere is scarred shut. Nothing crosses the wall in either direction, and all planar travel is severed — permanently.

The gods are outside

The gods are beyond the wall. They can see in, but they cannot touch, interact with, or influence anything inside the sphere.

The Fog of Life

From Toril's surface, neither sun nor stars are seen. The Fog of Life fills the sky — grey light and grey dark. The Sun still burns at the center; it is simply never seen.

Power is drawn, not granted

The gods' silence changes nothing about the placements. Inside the sphere, power is drawn, not granted — every world sits where the record put it.

The other worlds

In and of themselves, the other planets have not directly been touched by portals — no rifts opened on them. But incursions have been made by the demons, with Toril as the demons' home base for those incursions.

Stations of the sphere

The Rock of Bral — spelled B-R-A-L, pronounced “brawl” — still floats anchored above the Bastion, out in deep space off Toril's surface — a trading point between the cities. A couple of other stations exist besides it; their names and placements are not in the record.

The Spelljammer layer — canon in this sphere

Spelljammer is Shaun's standing exception, and it stands here in full: everything he has brought into the game — the ships and their deck plans, the helms, the peoples of space, the spells, the gear, and how it all works — is canon for this sphere and rides on this map. The APOC state still governs it all: the wall stays scarred shut, there is no planar travel, and there is no summoning. What sails, sails inside the sphere.

How it works

A ship flies because a helm drinks a spellcaster's power and turns it into motion. Around every hull rides an envelope of air the crew breathes; gravity settles along a plane through the ship, so decks hold "down" even in the void. Beyond the sphere wall lies the Phlogiston, the rainbow flow between spheres — sailed for ages before the Cut, and cut off now with everything else outside.

Under the Cut

The workings themselves never changed — helms still answer, air still holds, ships still ply wildspace between the worlds on the orrery above. What the Closing took is the way out: the old portal craft is kept in the record as written, but nothing crosses the scarred wall, in either direction, ever.

Deck plans — the plans kept with the ships

A ship on this map is not just a name: the plans of its decks are kept with it, sheet by sheet, ready to walk. Plans ride on the records of the Sprint Freighter, Dragonfly, Dragonship, Drakkar, Hammership, Nautiloid, Neogi Mindspider, Squid Ship, Tradesman, Wasp, Angelship, Archaic Man-o-War, Bloatfly, Cargo Barge, Cuttle Command, Deathglory, Dwarven Mining Ship, Eel Ship, Goblin Blade, Hummingbird, Lamprey Ship, Leaf Ship — whose sheet also covers the Thorn Ship — Mammoth, Mantis, Monarch Armada, Nightwolf, Octopus, Porcupine Ship, Pyramid Ship, Quad of Thay, Quentin's Libraria, Shrike, Skeleton Ship, Swan Ship, Turtle Ship, Unity Ship, Vipership, Vodoni Hunter-Killer, Vodoni Werewolf, and Wreckboat.

Some sheets belong to named vessels rather than classes: the Dragonfly Terigamar, the Drakkar Raven, the Hammership Barracuda, the Mindspider Collector, the Nautiloid Void Reaper, the Squid Ship Frostfire, the Vipership Mithril Serpent, and a drow Wasp. Shaun's own maps collection keeps plan sheets of its own — three for the DeathGlory (top deck; fore, flying and aft decks; cargo deck) and two for the Ogre Mammoth (cargo and upper decks; main and top decks), the only ships whose plans live in every keeping at once.

What the record has not settled

  • Where the dead go, now that the planes are cut.
  • The outcomes and extent of the demon incursions on the other worlds, world by world.
  • Whether anything has crossed the wall in a thousand years — nothing recorded says it has.

Placements, body types, and distances follow the published Realmspace record (SJR2). Post-apocalypse state is recorded only where the campaign record settles it; everywhere else the record is left honestly blank.

The Spelljammer layer is compiled from the campaign's own incorporated records: the boxed set (Shaun's copy of Spelljammer: Adventures in Space), the wider 2e line gathered into the game (Lost Ships, War Captain's Companion, The Legend of Spelljammer, The Astromundi Cluster, and more), Bastion Press Airships, Pirates!, Shaun's own homebrew ships and files, and the deck plans kept in his maps collection.

The Thousand Years

The Published Record Ends

Before the APOC

Forgotten Realms 3.5 history runs true and unbroken — then stops. Its last year is 1385 DR. Laney's career begins in 1386 DR, and runs 125 years.

Day One — The APOC

1511 DR

125 years after Laney began, the party fails in Limbo. The Demon God goes free. Laney is taken; her Oracle escapes before the door shuts. Rifts tear open — 80% of all life dead in 24 hours.

The Severing

The same moment

The crystal sphere scars shut — the Closing, the Cut. Planar travel ends forever. The gods are trapped outside, watching, unable to touch. Extra-dimensional magic and summoning die.

The Choices

The APOC era

The dragons withdraw under the Council of Wyrms — none seen in a thousand years. The drow choose the demons and are absorbed. The tieflings are taken, not willing.

Four Cities Hold

The APOC era

The Bastion of Last Hope — Waterdeep by its old name — Silverymoon, Sundabar, and a fourth city across the world, unknown even now. Citadel Adbar falls.

Alistair & the Levels

Early centuries (undated)

Alistair Blackstaff gives Levels 1–3 to the city, cordons Level 4 and below, seals breaches with his clones, builds stairs and hoists — then vanishes. The levels become city.

The New World's Shape

Early centuries (undated)

The Fog of Life lies grey and inert over the surface. Raw magic blights; alchemy becomes the cornerstone. The undead divide — unclaimed in the badlands, claimed in their Kingdom.

The Unwriting

~300 years in · c. 1811 DR

A demon-led undead army, a flying dracolich at its spear. A perfect sphere of the city unwritten from existence and memory. The city floods, holds — and builds the Heart of the City in the scoop. The sea in every open cavity turns the calamity inward — and the Mid Wall's great doors close for the first time.

The Long Middle

Years 300–900 (mostly undated)

A vertical city of 24 consortiums and temporary contracts. Guard outward, Watch inward. Shantytown razed and rebuilt, again and again. Reclamation crews walk the badlands. The Mid Wall's doors close only twice in all that time — the first was the Unwriting; the second, about 600 years later, was the city's fall to complete civil unrest.

The Second Closing

~600 years after the first · c. year 900 · c. 2411 DR

The city falls to complete civil unrest, and the Mid Wall's doors close for the second time — north sealed from south while the Bastion besieges itself.

Freedom, Second-Class

Within living memory

Slavery is abolished because of the Warforged. In the present it is about 1965 for the Warforged: free, and second-class.

The Present — 2511 DR

A thousand years after the door shut

Saint James, 70, in his 55th year pirating the badlands. The Southeast Brigade works south of the Mid Wall. A new party steps into the Bastion. Play begins.

What the record has not settled

  • No one now living can say when the Fog of Life first lay down over the world, nor when the Warforged first woke to themselves
  • Where the dead go, no one knows — the road to the Fugue has been cut for a thousand years
  • What became of Laney, and what the Demon God does now behind the closed sky, the record does not say
  • The fourth city — its name, its place, whether it stands or fell — is lost to everyone, even now
A thousand years, from the last page of the published world to the present day — as far as the record goes.

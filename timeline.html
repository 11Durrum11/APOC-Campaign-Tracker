Before the line

1385 DR — the published record ends. Forgotten Realms history, as published in the 3.5 materials, runs true and unbroken — and then it stops. Nothing published after that stands in this world. The line below starts the year after, with Laney’s career.

The Thousand Years

What the record has not settled

  • No one now living can say when the Fog of Life first lay down over the world, nor when the Warforged first woke to themselves
  • Where the dead go, no one knows — the road to the Fugue has been cut for a thousand years
  • What became of Laney, and what the Demon God does now behind the closed sky, the record does not say
  • Marrakesh rides somewhere beyond the far side of the world — where the horse carries it now, and whether it stands or has fallen, the record does not say
